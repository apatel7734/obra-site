Delete your Obra account

You can permanently delete your Obra account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app.

In the app

  1. Open Obra and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your account removes your projects, photos, voice memos, notes, reports, and your sign-in record.
