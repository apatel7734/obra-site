Common questions

How do I create a daily report?

Open a project, capture photos and voice memos or type notes for the day, then tap to generate the report. You can edit the text before sharing.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all of your projects, photos, notes, and reports.
